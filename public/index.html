What counts as a video

One finished video includes filming and editing to an agreed script. Content plans and scripts are included separately and do not count towards the video total.

Delivered in batches

First edits from each shooting day are typically ready in 2–3 days. Final delivery depends on feedback and the agreed revision schedule.

Up to 3 revision rounds

Per video, with one consolidated feedback list per round. Reshoots and changes to an approved script are discussed separately.

A shoot planned together

Video length, complexity and shooting-day hours are agreed before filming, based on the scripts and planned volume.

International shoots — quoted separately

International crew visits, flights, accommodation and local transport are not included in the package prices. We agree the scope and travel budget before booking.
